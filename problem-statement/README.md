# Problem statement

[Amazon-ML-Challenge-2026-Problem-Statement.pdf](Amazon-ML-Challenge-2026-Problem-Statement.pdf) is the official
problem statement of the **Amazon ML Challenge 2026: Business Entity Resolution Challenge** (8 pages). It is included
here for reference, unchanged. It belongs to the challenge organisers.

It covers:
- the task: match every Source 1 business to its records in Sources 2 and 3;
- the data files and their columns;
- the noise patterns (name and address variations);
- the two output files (`matching_results.tsv`, `candidate_pairs.tsv`) and their rules;
- the final submission package;
- the macro F0.5 metric with a worked example;
- the public and private leaderboards;
- the fair-play rules: no external data, and models MIT or Apache 2.0 licensed with ≤ 8 B parameters.

For a plain-language walkthrough of the same material, with more worked examples, see
[docs/problem.md](../docs/problem.md). The competition dataset is not included in this repository.
